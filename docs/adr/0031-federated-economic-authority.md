# ADR 0031: Federated economic authority and synthetic settlement

Status: contracts and three module source implementations in progress; deployment, scientific, browser and release acceptance pending.

Phase 13 cash-state statements remain immutable historical statements. They are not accounts, payments, or reservations. New independently installed Resource Availability, Mission Budget, and Resource Coordination providers own their respective observations/holds, budget versions/receipts/expenditures, and bilateral commitments/settlement reconciliation. No module edits another provider database. Master retains approved closed summaries, creates recommendations, and never approves or executes for a legal entity.

Original currencies use bounded integer minor units. The initial implementation supports USD/EUR at scale two, rejects cross-currency arithmetic, and supplies no exchange rates. Gross observed synthetic cash is reduced by disjoint obligations, restrictions, existing commitments, future requirements and the greater of absolute/expense-coverage reserve plus a liquidity buffer. Potential availability is neither legal distributability nor an approved commitment. Forecast earnings never enter current cash. Noncash capacity and documented/unknown valuation remain distinct from cash.

Policies and grants belong to the entity provider. Current authenticated identity and server-held grants, policy version, limits, expiration and separate requester/reviewer identities authorize immutable transaction digests. A policy revision, revocation or material amendment invalidates old authority. Receiving approval is independently performed at the receiving entity. Financial execution uses only a closed synthetic provider; no banking/payment integration or arbitrary endpoint exists. Pending/uncertain outcomes are reconciled by the original settlement identity; retries cannot manufacture another transfer.

The existing SSMA registry, approved workflows, pinned obligations, signed programme grants and durable inbox/outbox transport carry coordination. Financial dispatch and disclosure extend these mechanisms; legacy modules and old mission records remain supported. Financial and programme source references include entity/installation/module/stream/record/revision. No participant identities enter financial projections or MCP. Internal transfers retain both entity records but are excluded from new ecosystem income; receipts cannot count as both cash and noncash value. Journal evidence is bounded transaction evidence, not a complete production general ledger.

Scientific recommendations reuse the isolated Python service and require independent exact-integer reconciliation. They do not create obligations. Human-reviewed findings retain current source authority and affected-only delivery. All financial and service demonstrations are synthetic, with no live orders or external transfers.

Financial module calls receive a private, five-second Worker-host snapshot of enabled local administrators and enabled accounts with the specific module administration permission. Financial roles and limits remain in each module database. The module host replaces any caller-provided header with its own snapshot. Previous approvals cease to count if the account is disabled, demoted, loses module permission, loses programme authority, or its grant is revoked. More than 256 qualifying accounts fails closed rather than truncating authority. Peer execution also uses a receiving-entity permit bounded to 30 seconds; it is a short authorization lease, not an unbounded offline grant.

The Resource Availability provider owns source cash debits and original hold IDs. The Budget provider owns confirmed incoming receipts and spending. Coordination owns bilateral agreements, original simulation instruction IDs and references to provider journals; it does not create another cash ledger. Lost debit responses remain uncertain and reconcile against the original provider. An authoritative absent-reservation response writes an immutable fence so delayed reservation delivery cannot reopen that identity. A new proposal requires a new identity. A received intercompany contribution remains excluded from additional ecosystem income.

Noncash capacity explicitly distinguishes consumable quantities (including time budgets) from reusable physical facility/equipment units. Only bilateral observed returns of reusable units release consumed capacity. Valuation basis and operating costs are immutable proposal terms checked against the owning resource record; unknown value is never cash or a zero-cost assertion. Inactivating a route prevents new proposals but does not retarget old obligations.
