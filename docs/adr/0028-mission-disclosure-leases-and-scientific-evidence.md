# ADR 0028: Source programme disclosure leases and bounded mission intelligence

Status: implementation in progress; deployment and live-model acceptance are separate gates.

Master registry authority and legal-entity visibility do not authorize private programme evidence. A Worker administrator must also hold the module's current programme management and publication permissions. The authenticated module-owned `disclosureAuthority` operation checks those relationships. Core verifies the installed signed package's synthetic `programServices` declaration and signs a closed, named-Master-account lease using the enrolled Worker identity key. The proof pins Worker/entity/module/programme/domain, module installation/package, receiving Master/account, permission set, purpose, expiry and revision.

Leases and revocations use `steward.platform / mission_disclosures_v1` in the existing signed durable synchronization exchange. They are not arbitrary module events, case projections, or a new transport. Master verifies the enrolled public key, immutable grant scope and revision before storage. Every changed revision invalidates Master acceptance. The named account explicitly accepts the source-approved current revision; a registry administrator cannot accept another account's grant. A revoked grant cannot be restored by replay or renewal. A new independent grant requires a new source review.

The maximum lease is one hour. Revocation is effective at Master when its durable event arrives. During a network outage, authority is bounded by the remaining lease, rather than a claim of instantaneous remote revocation. Source programme administrators should revoke outstanding disclosures when changing an organization's sharing policy or responsible staff. Expired/revoked/changed leases hide retained calculations, scenarios, finding evidence, AI activity/history and parent context, including previously completed results. Historical evidence remains immutable internally.

Aggregate-only is the default stream scope. Including sealed financial authority statements requires a separate current module-owned check for programme management, publication and finance permission. A general aggregate publisher cannot export restricted finance records. The Worker periodically rechecks the original issuer, current programme authority and signed installed package/installation pins; it queues a signed durable revocation when those authority conditions change. Temporary unavailable authority checks retain only the existing bounded lease. Package pins identify the verified implementation authorizing the lease and are not silently rewritten during upgrades. All received disclosure source metadata is closed and typed; arbitrary object-valued names/configuration fields are rejected before persistence.

Mission evidence is isolated from general `/api/v4/analytics` and legacy entity-scoped scientific datasets/runs. New programme routes resolve exact `(entity, Worker, module, stream, record, revision)` references and recheck all source programmes. An origin funding programme and recipient service programme require independent source-issued authority. No arbitrary participant dimensions, date windows, cohort recombination or private case retrieval is offered. The model sees compact fixed-source summaries while complete approved inputs and scientific results remain retained under the same current permissions. General findings readers also enforce programme access. Delivery targets only Workers named by source evidence; local programme managers must hold current module authority to read or acknowledge mission findings.

Private scenario/calculation inputs are owner-only; publishing a reviewed finding creates a separately scoped derived summary rather than opening the owner's private planning history. Reference/value pairs retain their complete source identity throughout storage, AI context and delivery. Deliberate UUID collisions fail closed instead of attaching money to another module or Worker. Reusing one source organization's financial authority under different allocation IDs cannot create extra money. Every structured tool result, including generic historical findings with snake-case references, is recursively authorized before logging/model use and again on retained-history/context reuse. Ordinary business MCP findings omit mission findings entirely. A same-entity Worker that never received a finding cannot acknowledge it.

Programme modules own private financial authority records. A draft records explicit synthetic organizational approval evidence, restrictions, stable allocation/authority/transfer identities and exact-cent authorized/available/committed/transferred/spent amounts. An explicit financial reviewer seals an immutable canonical-period authority/cash-state statement. Private approval narrative does not replicate. Duplicate approval or transfer identities cannot create another availability claim. Recipient mirrors do not add funds. These records do not execute external payments. A sealed statement is not silently edited to represent later transactions; supported later accounting workflows require separately governed periods/authorities and reconciliation.

Period resource gap compares observed period operating cost with explicitly authorized resources for that period. The current already-net available balance is used only for additional conditional expansion. Transfers, commitments and spending are displayed separately, never subtracted twice. Missing/suppressed measures remain unknown. Commerce revenue or projected profit never becomes programme funding automatically.

Human-reviewed scenarios declare unit costs, service ratios and bounded capacities. TypeScript computes exact integer-cent bounds. Scientific service **1.1.0** independently solves the same bounded integer service-bundle problem using SciPy/HiGHS, with a two-second solver bound, one thread, fixed seed and independent exact reconciliation. Service units are conditional planning quantities, not unique people, participant eligibility, service delivery, reservations or financial commitments. Shared source provenance defaults remain unchanged for historical Phase 12 evidence. The existing scientific service transport, authentication, process supervision and concurrency limits are reused.

The mission AI profile discovers permitted programmes and existing human-reviewed scenarios, selects a scenario by observed ID and invokes the deterministic service. It cannot supply new costs, capacities, sources or grant permissions. Results require exact service-formatted financial amounts and explicit human decisions. Finding publication is separate human review and uses retained deterministic evidence. A test-model protocol fixture is reproducible coverage, never evidence of a real model investigation.

Phase 13 declarative packages may contain at most 96 API operations and 20 screens, accommodating shared programme/case administration, financial review and operational screens. Legacy limits remain 30 operations/10 screens. This is a bounded signed-data allowance; arbitrary scripts, assets and permissions remain forbidden. The independently packaged module renderer is preserved.

Limitations include synthetic-only participants, conservative suppression rather than differential privacy, explicit manual account/programme approval setup, bounded lease revocation latency during outages, small fixed-cohort histories, and immutable statement-based financial demonstration rather than a complete accounting/payment system. These choices do not establish HMIS certification, privacy-law compliance, clinical suitability or commercial production readiness.
