# ADR 0017 — Approved canonical business evidence and model views

Status: accepted; final Phase 7 release verification is in progress.

Master continues to ingest the existing authenticated, versioned synchronization envelopes. Modules own operational records and their projection tables. Procurement publishes approved external demand and supplier quotes, plus operational purchase commitments. Sales contributes order demand and fulfillment constraints. Inventory contributes item quantities and replenishment states. Assets contributes approved capacity snapshots with explicit owner/custodian/user/access relationships and scheduled validity boundaries. Workforce contributes expressly approved aggregate skill supply, training metrics and job demand; its person, enrollment and achievement tables are absent from replication and MCP declarations.

New canonical contracts do not name particular companies or installations. The existing Master analytical capability registry and MCP server resolve authorized source IDs and revisions, then perform deterministic calculations. Cross-module resource matching uses canonical stream meaning, rather than requiring the supplying and demanding modules to have identical IDs. Expired asset capacity snapshots are excluded until refreshed by their owning module. Operational authority remains at the Worker.

Shared procurement uses exact integer cents, observed quantities/prices/shipping, one combined external quote and each approved transition expense once. Internal transfers cancel at ecosystem level. Incompatible units/currencies, expired quotes, insufficient quoted quantities and integer overflow are rejected. Workforce calculations retain suppressed counts as unknown, never add overlapping people and completions, and do not infer complete qualification when additional required skills lack evidence. Training seats describe future capacity, not graduates or hiring decisions.

The existing Analyst accepts an optional capability profile; omitted capability preserves the resource-sharing workflow. Profiles narrow offered read-only tools and provide short planning guidance without preselecting source records, supplying tool results or executing business actions. Genuine model calls still discover and invoke authenticated MCP tools. Full tool responses, arguments, source records and calculation outputs remain in activity storage. Compact provider views contain only relevant evidence.

Real Qwen testing exposed a cents-versus-major-currency wording error. The analytical service now formats currency strings exactly from integer cents using integer arithmetic. Compact procurement finalization receives these unambiguous display amounts while the original exact-cent evidence remains retained. This avoids asking the model to perform unit conversion. The change applies to every model adapter.

Model text remains subject to operator review. Phase 7 live verification defers finding publication until a separate reviewed browser action; the model has no finding publication tool. A synthetic finding produced by the legacy verifier before wording review was explicitly closed, with closure delivery verified. Failed calls, rejected wording and source evidence are retained rather than presented as successful acceptance.

Limits: this is not currency conversion, accounting, payroll, recruiting automation or an authorization to purchase/hire. Module data uses a configured currency and documented units; changing the meaning of populated operational quantities/currencies is outside this release. Commercial terms, allocations, consent, suitability and execution remain human decisions.
