# ADR 0042: Reviewed Catalog intelligence retains original Worker evidence

Status: implemented for Phase 15G; final deployment and release acceptance pending.

Catalog, Pricing and Sales remain the operational authorities. Master receives
only explicitly reviewed, closed, bounded analytical publications through the
existing signed durable synchronization protocol. Installing a module does not
automatically disclose its operational tables. Publications expire or are
withdrawn without deleting their original history. Local operations remain
independent of Master and scientific service availability.

The four versioned streams separate Catalog definitions, Pricing summaries,
split immutable Pricing evidence, and accepted Sales attribution. Each reference
includes entity, Worker installation, module, stream, record and revision. Provider
installation and signed package identity remain in the payload. Equal IDs,
names, SKUs or labels across Workers never establish a shared identity. These
streams are excluded from legacy analytics whose source references lack Worker
identity; dedicated bounded endpoints enforce fresh account scope before lookup,
search and pagination.

Pricing publication requires a separate human review under the existing approval
policy. Summary/evidence counts and digests bind the complete source set. Master
recomputes integer-cent contribution from original observations, units, allocation,
price and approval evidence before comparison. Unknown costs or origin coverage
remain unknown and unranked. Compatible cohorts retain currency, quantity, full
unit proof, acquisition, cost scope and pricing context. Local price-list UUIDs
are provenance rather than cross-company product identity. Promotional and
customer-specific contexts retain their local scope. No ecosystem profit total,
price activation, purchasing commitment or provider mutation is authorized.

Sales retains immutable accepted prices. Human attribution after acceptance does
not claim that Pricing was used for the original transaction. Current analysis
requires the original exact Catalog classification to remain available; withdrawn
Sales publications still retain their historical classification evidence. Reported
fulfilled merchandise values are cumulative accepted-line values, not complete
period accounting revenue. Refund allocation and actual contribution stay unknown.

The existing Python scientific engine accepts only server-materialized values
from exact authorized sources. Request/result digests, source provenance and
descriptive statistics are independently reconciled. The established Python UTC
normalization is matched without altering its contract. Exact mean fractions
avoid rounding statistical values into transaction amounts. Scope and source
leases are rechecked after asynchronous calculation. Failures persist no successful
calculation. Immutable calculation records retain full Worker references alongside
the unchanged scientific provenance contract.

Accepted-line quantity descriptions require distinct original lines for the same
exact offering revision and channel. Missing orders or days are not zero. These
descriptions are neither a complete demand time series nor a forecast or causal
promotion analysis. Cross-company supplier identity, operational compatibility and
commercial actions require additional explicit evidence and human review.

Master presentation uses SSDS reports and charts with server-owned aggregation.
Operational module screens continue through the generic modular renderer.
Migration 19 appends immutable analytical calculation storage; existing applied
migrations and operational module data are preserved.
