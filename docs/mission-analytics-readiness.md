# Mission analytics readiness (Phase 12)

Steward's commercial contracts remain neutral. A legal entity may operate housing,
food services, training, employment, manufacturing or an unrelated commercial
business. Classification does not grant disclosure or operational authority.
Phase 12 processes no real sensitive participant information and creates no case
management subsystem.

Existing foundations include entity-scoped assets/capacity and resource-demand
observations, aggregate synthetic workforce/training/skill opportunities,
currency-separated commerce contributions, approved legal/operational
relationships, source-revision synchronization, governed findings and human
acknowledgments. Scientific capabilities consume bounded authorized snapshots
through the same analytical/MCP path. Observed business contribution is neither
available charitable cash nor a mission-impact measure: taxes, working capital,
restricted funds, legal obligations and allocation consent remain separate.

Future independently packaged modules can contribute aggregate measures such as
available housing nights, food-service capacity and meals, training seats and
verified aggregate completions, supported employment capacity and sustained
housing/service outcomes. Their signed capabilities should declare units, period,
population/cohort definition, observed versus estimated basis, completeness,
minimum disclosure thresholds, sensitivity, source stream/record/revision,
retention and owner approval. The existing SSMA registry/resolver, entity scope,
stream-scoped source references, dedicated module databases and human workflow
approvals are the extension points; no parallel registry is needed.

Sensitive modules would require a separately reviewed privacy and authorization
design before implementation: data minimization, purpose limitation, consent,
small-cohort suppression, retention/deletion, protected attributes and appropriate
legal/clinical review. Avoid direct or indirect identification through combinations
of aggregate outputs. Current synthetic aggregate skill data does not authorize
collecting real participant records or determining individual eligibility.

Mission evaluation must distinguish inputs, services delivered, outcomes, equity,
quality, uncertainty and counterfactual assumptions. Do not equate short-term
profit, revenue, an internal transfer or a forecast with humanitarian impact.
Financial support scenarios require explicit entity consent and restricted-fund
constraints; recommendations cannot authorize a transfer, hiring decision,
housing placement or consequential individual action. Commercial customers may
use all business analytics without adopting CEE mission measures.
