# Phase 14 financial source checkpoint

These modules are unpublished 1.0.0 source implementations requiring platform 0.14.0. The selected operator remains the verified 0.13 deployment. The procedures below describe module API/declarative UI lifecycles exercised in isolated PostgreSQL tests; browser and deployed transport acceptance are pending.

Each programme has a separately activated entity policy, current named actors, delegated grants and immutable review evidence. A distinct reviewer activates the policy and approves authored terms. The configured quorum counts different currently authorized humans, never repeated clicks. Editing a policy invalidates prior policy-scoped grants/reviews. Its activation quorum cannot be weakened by the proposed edit. Master recommendations confer no financial authority.

Resource Availability owns immutable opening synthetic cash and separately reviewed cash adjustments. Observed cash is reduced by disjoint obligations, restricted balances, external commitments, future requirements, internal holds and the greater of absolute or operating-coverage reserve plus buffer. Approval is an assessment only; distributability is not asserted. New assessments cannot reset prior holds. Current cash availability is unavailable when evidence, account authority, policy or reviews are stale. Reviewed reversals append a referenced opposite journal; original records remain unchanged.

Mission Budget separates approved plans, requested funding, accepted commitments, confirmed receipts, encumbrances and posted spending. Draft lines lock on submission. Revisions retain old versions and preserve funded restrictions and outstanding obligations. Confirmed receipts alone become spendable funds. Posting requires current distinct reviews and a delegated executor, and consumes an encumbrance exactly once. A referenced expense reversal retains its original journal and does not recreate already released encumbrances. Conditional funded capacity deducts remaining minimum operating requirements; it is not evidence of delivered services.

Resource Coordination pins each proposal to an original owner, source/receiving programme, assessment, policy version, immutable terms digest and verified provider receipts. Source reservation and approval precede submission. The receiver independently reviews and, for cash, accepts an existing approved budget request. The source executor obtains a fresh receiving permit before requesting consumption. Only the configured `steward.synthetic-settlement` provider exists. No external endpoint, payment credential or live transfer facility is accepted.

After a provider confirms the original debit, its settlement evidence is sent to the receiver. A human records receipt through Budget; the source reconciles the same settlement and actual receipt ID. Pending instructions and commitments are not receipts. Lost responses require reconciliation of the original identity, not a second debit. A late rejection cannot overwrite an independently confirmed debit. A failed initial reservation can be reconciled to a permanently fenced absence, after which a new proposal is required.

Cancellation after acceptance requires receiver approval and cancellation of the unreceived Budget commitment before the source releases its original hold. Settled/uncertain obligations cannot be blindly cancelled. Amendments link a new proposal to the cancelled original. Noncash has separate delivery identities and never creates a cash receipt. Consumable time/capacity stays consumed. Reusable physical units require receiving return observation and owner confirmation before becoming available again. Shared resource screens show held, consumed and currently available quantities separately.

Original USD and EUR values use integer minor units, with no implicit conversion. Private balanced journals retain entity gross activity and classify internal/intercompany movements separately. They are bounded transaction evidence, not a production general ledger. No participant identifiers, private banking details or live commercial transactions belong in these synthetic records.

Disputed receiving obligations remain frozen until an immutable evidence-based resolution is separately reviewed and applied by a current receiver. It resumes the same factual original state and terms only. Subsequent execution still requires current source approvals and a fresh receiver permit. Factual callbacks cannot silently remove the dispute.

Reviewed summary publication, named-account Master access, closed signed synchronization and durable invalidation now pass source/API tests. Source records invalidate older sealed copies transactionally; a new reviewed publication is needed before those values can fund another calculation. The Master view distinguishes current-as-of, historical and invalidated evidence.

Remaining gates include deployed/browser recovery and reporting UX, mission cost-source integration, governed scientific scenarios from sealed sources, proposal-to-Worker drafts, MCP/Qwen, normal-browser demonstration, real deployment/outage/upgrade/clean-install acceptance and release packaging. No source checkpoint establishes Phase 14 completion.
